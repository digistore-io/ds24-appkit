## Where it is

*Account* in the menu on the left, and also behind your name in the top right.
It is your own page — it shows your account and nobody else's.

## What it shows

- **What you may use**, with an end date where there is one. "No end date" means
  it runs until it is cancelled or ends by itself.
- **Your balance**, if this app sells prepaid credit. Apps that sell only
  subscriptions do not show this.
- **Your sign-in details** — email address and, optionally, a password.

## What you can change

**Your email address.** You enter the new one and we send a confirmation link to
it. Nothing changes until you follow that link, so a typo costs you nothing:
request it again with the correct address and the old link stops working. Your
old address keeps working for signing in until then, and everything on your
account — balance, plans, history — stays with you when the address changes.

**Your password.** You can set one, change it or remove it at any time. There is
no password reset by email, and none is needed: if you forget it, sign in with
the emailed link as before and set a new one.

Every change to your password sends you a notice by email. That notice never
contains a link — if you get one that does, it is not from us.
