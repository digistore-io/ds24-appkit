## What this app is

You are signed in to a web app you reached through a purchase or an invitation.
Everything you have access to sits behind the sign-in; nothing here is public.

## The first five minutes

1. **Open the overview.** It is the first entry in the menu on the left and
   shows what is currently active on your account.
2. **Check your account page.** It is the second entry in the menu on the left
   and lists what you may use, until when, and — if this app sells credit —
   your balance.
3. **Set a password if you want one.** Signing in works by emailed link without
   one. A password is optional and saves you the trip through your inbox. It is
   on the account page, under the sign-in section.

## If something looks wrong

A purchase that has just gone through can take a moment to appear: the payment
provider notifies the app, and that notification arrives seconds to minutes
after you pay. Reload the page once. If it is still missing after a few minutes,
contact support with your order number — it is in the confirmation email.
