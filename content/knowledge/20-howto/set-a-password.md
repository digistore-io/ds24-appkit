## Before you start

You need to be signed in. If you are not, sign in the usual way first — enter
your email address and follow the link we send you.

## Setting one

1. Open *Account* from the menu on the left.
2. Scroll to the sign-in section.
3. Enter the new password twice and save.

You will get an email confirming that a password was set. That is expected, and
it is your warning if it was not you.

## Changing or removing one

Same page, same section. Removing it puts the account back to signing in by
emailed link only, which is how it worked before — nothing is lost.

## If you forgot it

There is no reset link, and you do not need one. On the sign-in screen, choose
**send me a link instead**, sign in through your inbox, and set a new password
on the account page.
